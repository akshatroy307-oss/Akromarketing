About AkroMarketing

A modern agency with roots in Bihar and a global client focus.

AkroMarketing is a web development and digital marketing agency founded by Akshat Roy and based in Darbhanga, Bihar. We help businesses build a stronger digital presence through modern web development, creative branding, and strategic digital marketing.

Our approach is founder-led and client-focused. We aim to serve businesses across India and the United States, from local businesses to ambitious brands in Texas, New York City, and San Francisco.
